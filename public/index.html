NOTES FROM THE TEAM-BUILDING JOURNEY

The Starglow journal.

Practical ideas and approachable guides to help you understand your characters, build cohesive teams, and make the most of your adventures across Teyvat.

TEAM BUILDING · THE FUNDAMENTALS

Build around a plan, not just powerful characters

A strong team is more than four individually impressive characters. Start by deciding what your on-field character needs, then choose teammates who provide the right reactions, buffs, healing, protection, or energy.

Think of each slot as having a purpose. When the pieces support one another, a team often feels smoother and more reliable than a lineup assembled around rarity alone.

Try this: Pick your main damage dealer, identify how they deal damage, and fill the remaining slots with support that helps them do it consistently.
ROLES & SYNERGY

Give every team slot a job

Before choosing a teammate, ask what your team is missing. You may need off-field damage, elemental application, a healer, a shielder, a buffer, or a battery to help with Burst uptime.

Not every team needs every role. The best combination depends on the characters you own and the way you want to play.

COMBAT FLOW

Make your rotation feel natural

A rotation is a repeatable order for setting up your team and using its abilities. In general, apply support effects first, prepare reactions and buffs, then bring in your main damage dealer while those effects are active.

Treat any suggested rotation as a starting point. Adjust it for cooldowns, Energy needs, enemy movement, and the duration of your buffs.

A NOTE FOR EVERY ACCOUNT

Build with the roster you have

You do not need every limited character to make a capable team. Familiar four-star characters, free options, and flexible supports can form a dependable foundation.

Experiment with what you have, learn why a combination works, and improve it gradually as your roster grows.

Starglow journal articles are general, fan-made guidance. Character kits, game balance, and available content may change over time.

Hello, I’m Arthur.

I’m a creator based in the Philippines, and I built Starglow to make team-building in Genshin Impact more approachable and enjoyable.

My goal is to help players make informed choices with the characters they already have, understand how those characters work together, and create effective teams they can continue using throughout their journey in the game.

Starglow brings roster planning, teammate recommendations, and rotation guidance into one place. It is designed to support experimentation and help players find team combinations that suit their own playstyle and progress.
